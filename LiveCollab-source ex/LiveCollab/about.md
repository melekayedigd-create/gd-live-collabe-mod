# Live Collab

Build Geometry Dash levels **together with a friend, live**.

1. One player presses **Host** in the main menu and gets a 6-letter room code.
2. The other player presses **Join** and types that code.
3. Both open a level in the editor. The host's level appears on the friend's screen,
   and every block placed, moved, edited or deleted shows up for both players.

Works over the internet (Wi-Fi or mobile data) through a free public relay server - no port forwarding.
